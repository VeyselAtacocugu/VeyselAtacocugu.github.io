Beyin Tuzagi Fabrika

The channel owner's private publishing tool for @beyintuzagiofficial.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account below (Login Kit). We only read your display name to confirm it is your own account.
  2. Paste the one-time code into the Fabrika Connect form. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  3. In the Fabrika Publisher form you see the connected account, pick your video, write the caption, choose who can view it, set comments / Duet / Stitch and the content disclosure, agree to the Music Usage Confirmation and press Run. Each post is labelled AI-generated.
